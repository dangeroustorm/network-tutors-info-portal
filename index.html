4-Step Router Connection Guide

Follow these 4 steps to set up your router in Wohnanlage Chiemgauer Str.

Connect Power Adapter & Blue WAN Port

Plug the power adapter into an outlet and press the power button. Connect the network cable from your room's wall socket directly into the BLUE WAN Port on the back of your router.

Network Safety Tip: Connecting your wall cable to the blue WAN port helps ensure a smooth, stable connection for your room and prevents local network loops on your floor.
Click any port on the router diagram to inspect its function.
Router Rear Panel Overview

Connect via Default Wi-Fi Network

Turn on Wi-Fi on your laptop or smartphone. Connect using the default Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and wireless password printed on the sticker at the back or bottom of your router.

Default SSID (Wi-Fi Name)
See sticker on back/bottom of router
Example: TP-Link_XXXX
Default Wireless Password
Check the 8-digit PIN or Wireless Password printed on the router sticker.

Router Admin Dashboard (Optional)

To customize your Wi-Fi name or password, open a web browser and go to http://192.168.0.1 or tplinkwifi.net. Use the default login credentials printed on your router's sticker or manual.

Router Access Address
http://192.168.0.1
Alternative: tplinkwifi.net
Default Admin Password
Refer to the sticker on the back of your router or user manual.

WAN & DHCP Configuration (Optional)

If prompted in the setup wizard, choose Dynamic IP (DHCP) as your connection type. Note: Additional step-by-step setup documents were sent to your email prior to your move-in date.

Quick Setup Summary:
  • Click Quick Setup in the left navigation menu.
  • Choose Wireless Router mode.
  • WAN Connection Type: Select Dynamic IP (Do NOT choose PPPoE or Static IP).
  • MAC Clone: Keep Do NOT clone MAC address.
Tip: For detailed step-by-step configuration guides, please refer to the orientation documents sent to your email address prior to move-in.

Network Security Rules

Please follow dormitory network security guidelines to prevent service interruption and legal issues.

Essential Security Guidelines

Unified network rules for Wohnanlage Chiemgauer Str. residents

No Rogue DHCP Servers: Always connect the wall cable exclusively to the blue WAN port to avoid local network loops.
No Copyright Infringement (BitTorrent / P2P): Peer-to-peer file sharing of copyrighted media is strictly monitored in Germany. Infringements lead to heavy legal fines.
Router Password Security: Change your router admin password from the default setting to prevent unauthorized modifications.
Personal Residential Use Only: Bandwidth is for academic research and residential internet use. Commercial hosting or reselling connectivity is forbidden.
