Network Safety & Guidelines

Essential Dormitory Network Rules

  • Always connect the wall Ethernet outlet strictly to the BLUE WAN port of your router.
  • Avoid plugging wall cables into yellow LAN ports to prevent network loops that might disrupt internet access for your floor.
  • Keep your router administrator password updated and secure to prevent unauthorized network access.
  • Adhere strictly to LRZ acceptable usage policies regarding copyright, legal compliance, and network safety.
